Head of Investment & Operations
Make the CFO/COO faster across investment and operations.
Reports to: CFO/COO

Act as the senior execution partner to the CFO/COO across investment analysis, underwriting, financing, operations and executive priorities. Combine the analytical responsibilities of the Investment Analyst with the execution and coordination responsibilities of Operations.

Key responsibilities
  • Lead and oversee deal underwriting and financial analysis.
  • Build and stress-test investment and deal models.
  • Evaluate acquisition opportunities and investment returns.
  • Analyze debt, assumable loans, non-recourse, JV, LP/GP and private-equity structures.
  • Model financing alternatives and determine structures that maximize returns.
  • Monitor financial and operational performance.
  • Identify where NOI is leaking and develop solutions.
  • Research markets, financing conditions and competitors.
  • Source and coordinate attorneys, lenders, brokers, underwriters and property managers.
  • Keep external partners and vendors moving.
  • Manage critical follow-ups and action items.
  • Prepare executive meetings, briefings and decision materials.
  • Support the CFO/COO across priorities and business execution.
  • Bring research, analysis and solutions proactively.
  • Use AI and technology to improve investment and operational workflows.
What we’re looking for
  • Strong financial analysis and modeling skills.
  • Commercial real estate experience; multifamily strongly preferred.
  • Strong understanding of financing and investment structures.
  • Combination of finance and operations experience.
  • Excellent organizational and execution skills.
  • Strong written and verbal English.
  • Confidence dealing directly with lenders, brokers and attorneys.
  • Self-starter who owns outcomes.
  • Ability to move quickly and operate with judgment.
